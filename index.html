Los Gatos Math Circle

Students in grades 6 to 12 meet at the Los Gatos Library on Saturday mornings to work on hard problems together. It's free and student-run. You can drop in any week.

From our first meeting of 2025–26

The locker problem

A hallway has 1000 closed lockers. Student 1 opens every locker. Student 2 toggles every second locker, student 3 toggles every third, and so on up to student 1000.

When everyone is done, how many lockers are open?

Hint

Locker n is toggled once for every divisor of n.

Answer

31. Divisors of n pair up as d and n/d, so almost every locker is toggled an even number of times. The exception is a perfect square, where √n pairs with itself. There are 31 squares up to 1000, since 31² = 961 and 32² = 1024.

A Saturday

We start with a short warm-up puzzle, then split into three groups by experience. Explorers (grades 6 to 8) work on puzzles and patterns with no contest background needed. Challengers (8 to 10) work AMC-style problems and first proofs. Olympians (10 to 12) prepare for the AIME and ARML.

Most of the morning goes to one themed problem set. In the last half hour people present solutions at the board, and everyone leaves with one harder problem for the week.
