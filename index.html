Welcome to the Los Gatos Math Circle

The Los Gatos Math Circle is a free, student-run math circle for students in grades 6 to 12. We meet on Saturday mornings during the school year in the Teen Section of the Los Gatos Library, 100 Villa Avenue. Each session is built around one topic (combinatorics, geometry, number theory, algebra, invariants) and most of the time goes to working problems in small groups and presenting solutions at the board.

No contest experience is needed, and there is no application for regular sessions. You can come to any meeting. If you would like us to save you a seat, email hello@losgatosmathcircle.org.

Problem of the week

The locker problem

A hallway has 1000 closed lockers. Student 1 opens every locker. Student 2 toggles every second locker, student 3 toggles every third, and so on up to student 1000. When everyone is done, how many lockers are open?

Hint

Locker n is toggled once for every divisor of n.

Answer

31. Divisors of n pair up as d and n/d, so most lockers are toggled an even number of times. Perfect squares are the exception, since √n pairs with itself. There are 31 squares up to 1000 (31² = 961, 32² = 1024).

From our first meeting of the 2025–26 season.
