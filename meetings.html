Meetings

We meet Saturday mornings during the school year, in the Teen Section of the Los Gatos Library. It's free, and you don't need to sign up. Just come.

Where we meet

The Teen Section is on the lower level of the library. If it's your first time, walk in and grab a seat. Someone will catch you up on the problem we're working on.

  • Address100 Villa Avenue, Los Gatos, CA 95030
  • RoomTeen Section, lower level
  • TimeSaturdays, 10 AM to noon
  • SeasonSeptember to May
  • ParkingThe library lot on Villa Ave, or street parking
  • CostFree
  • BringA pencil and scratch paper

Questions? Email hello@losgatosmathcircle.org.
